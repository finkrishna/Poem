भज गोविन्दम्

Worship Govinda

आदि शङ्कराचार्य · Ādi Śaṅkara   with verses of the disciples after the first twelve

Shankara’s hammer of delusion: seek Govinda, not grammar, wealth, or the body. The first twelve verses are his; a colophon follows, then verses of the disciples. This page is the first twenty of the usual recension. The refrain भज गोविन्दं is sung after each verse; here it stands as verse 1.

English

Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, O deluded mind.

When the hour of death is upon you, the rules of grammar will not save you.

Note

The story: Shankara meets an aged scholar in Varanasi drilling Pāṇini. डुकृङ्करणे is a technical grammar formula, standing here for mere book-learning.

Good company gives rise to non-attachment; non-attachment, to freedom from delusion.

That freedom becomes a still truth — and from that stillness, liberation in this very life.

Note

A chain of cause: satsaṅga → nissaṅgatva → nirmoha → niścala-tattva → jīvanmukti.

English

In these twelve flower-clusters the whole counsel has been spoken to the grammarian.

The teaching came from the blessed Śaṅkara, master of knowledge, whose refuge is the Lord.

Note

Colophon of the first twelve verses (the dvādaśa-mañjarikā), Shankara’s own. The verses that follow are traditionally those of his disciples.
