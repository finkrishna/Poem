श्रावण मासी

The month of Shravan

बालकवी · Balakavi   Tryambak Bapuji Thombre (1890–1918)

Balakavi’s poem of the monsoon month: showers that come and go in a moment, a rainbow over the sky, white birds in flight, a cowherd’s flute, and women setting out with flowers and leaves for worship.

English

In Shravan, the heart rejoices; green crowds every view.

A shower sweeps through—and in a breath, the sunlight comes anew.

Note

Shravan is a month in the Hindu calendar associated with the monsoon. “सरसर” gives the shower its quick, rustling movement.

At the sight of parijat, even Bhama’s anger fades.

Note

Bhama is Satyabhama, associated with Krishna and the celestial parijat tree. The line evokes her anger being soothed at its sight.
